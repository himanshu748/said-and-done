# Said & Done privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Said & Done is a Forge app for Jira and Confluence Cloud, provided free by Himanshu Jha.

**What it stores.** For each promise it detects: the sentence, the action and due date it read from it, the Atlassian account ID and display name of the writer and of the owner, a link to the issue or page, its status and a short history. This is kept in Forge storage on Atlassian's infrastructure for your site.

**What it reads.** Jira comments, Confluence pages and page comments, to look for promises; user profiles, for names and time zones.

**What it writes.** Reminder comments on the issue or page where the promise was made.

**What leaves your site.** Nothing. The app has no external network access, uses no AI service, analytics or third-party processor, and the developer cannot see your data.

**Deletion.** Uninstalling the app deletes its stored data, as Forge does for all apps. Marking a promise "dropped" keeps it in the ledger; contact us to remove one entirely.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/himanshu748/said-and-done/issues.
